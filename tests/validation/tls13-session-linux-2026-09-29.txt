  - 50000-byte replies split differently and delivered through 137-byte reads.
  - 100 OpenSSL request/response exchanges with 3-byte reads and close_notify.
  - Replay, tag alteration, premature EOF, unsupported post-handshake messages,
   oversized record headers, and excessive empty records fail closed.
  - Output unchanged on rejected records; subsequent I/O returns Closed.
  - Disconnected writes return errors without SIGPIPE process termination.
  - Empty request/response records, zero-capacity empty response, and oversized
   response rejection preserve the legacy round-trip boundary.
  - Two explicitly aligned allocations and exactly two frees after Drop or
