DHE-specific guarantees exercised
  Independent peer parses the ClientHello extension vectors, requires only
  psk_dhe_ke and X25519, and verifies PSK-last placement and the PSK binder.
  Python X25519 independently derives the shared secret; HMAC/HKDF derives
  traffic secrets and verifies the client's Finished and application record.
  The rejected ServerHello/handshake cases emit no client Finished or
  application data and leave caller output unchanged.
  Null private input and private bytes equal to public ClientHello random
  (same pointer or distinct buffers) are refused before allocation or I/O.
  Private inputs are separate OS-CSPRNG samples and remain unchanged.
  OpenSSL runs with -groups X25519 and without -allow_no_dhe_kex.
  Two aligned allocations are released exactly once in each peer case.
